# Investor Eligibility Criteria - Draft

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Eligible Investor Definitions
- Accredited: meets income or net worth thresholds per jurisdiction.
- Qualified: meets local suitability or sophistication tests.
- Institutional: regulated entities with internal risk governance.

## Required Evidence (Off-chain)
- Identity verification and AML screening.
- Jurisdiction confirmation and sanctions screening.
- Accreditation or qualification verification where required.

## On-chain Representation
- Eligibility stored as boolean status.
- Investor type and jurisdiction stored as hashes.
- No personal data stored on-chain.
