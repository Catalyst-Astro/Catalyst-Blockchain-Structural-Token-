# Jurisdiction Restrictions - Draft

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Regulatory Basis
- Mexico: Ley del Mercado de Valores (private offering rules).
- United States: Regulation D and Regulation S.

## Reg D
- Rule 506(b): no general solicitation; verified accredited investors.
- Rule 506(c): allowed solicitation with strict accreditation verification.

## Reg S
- Offshore offering only.
- No directed selling efforts into the United States.

## On-chain Control
- Allowed jurisdictions encoded as hashes.
- Transfers require jurisdiction checks via eligibility registry.
