# Operational Model - Private Offering

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## 1) Onboarding Process
- Verify eligibility and accreditation where required.
- Verify jurisdiction and sanctions screening.
- Record acceptance of disclosures and subscription agreement.

## 2) Subscription Agreement
- Signed off-chain via e-signature or clickwrap.
- Hash stored on-chain via PrivateOfferingRegistry.

## 3) Primary Allocation
- Token distribution only to eligible wallets.
- Apply lockup and transfer restriction policy.

## 4) Secondary Transfer Control
- Transfers only to eligible wallets.
- Jurisdiction checks enforced.
- DAO council approval for exceptions if required.

## 5) Evidence and Audit Trail
- Maintain document hashes and change logs.
- Retain off-chain evidence in secure storage.
