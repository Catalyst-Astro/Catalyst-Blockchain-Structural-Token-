# Marketing Compliance Policy - Draft

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Allowed Claims
- Private offering only.
- Economic rights token with compliance restrictions.
- Eligibility and verification required.

## Prohibited Claims
- Guaranteed yield or fixed return.
- Public sale or open participation.
- Unlimited liquidity or instant exit.

## Communication Controls
- Pre-approved messaging only.
- No mass solicitation or public advertising.
- Maintain audit logs of marketing materials.
