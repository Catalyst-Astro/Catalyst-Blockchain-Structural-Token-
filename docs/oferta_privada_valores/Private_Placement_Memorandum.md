# Private Placement Memorandum (PPM) - Draft

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Project Summary
Fractal Token represents economic rights to distributions from trust-held assets. The token does not convey direct ownership of real estate or tourism assets. Distributions depend on asset performance and governance decisions.

## Token Nature
- Economic right to proportional distributions.
- Not legal tender or a general payment instrument.
- Not a deposit, credit, or guaranteed yield product.

## Risk Factors
- Market risk: asset and tourism revenue volatility.
- Execution risk: delays or operational failures.
- Regulatory risk: changes in law or interpretation.
- Smart contract risk: defects or integration failures.
- Liquidity risk: limited secondary market and transfer restrictions.
- Force majeure: events beyond control affecting distributions.

## Use of Proceeds
Funds are allocated to asset acquisition, operations, compliance, and platform security.

## Transfer Restrictions
- Private offering only.
- Transfers limited to verified eligible investors.
- Jurisdiction and lockup restrictions apply.
- Secondary transfers require compliance approval and on-chain checks.
