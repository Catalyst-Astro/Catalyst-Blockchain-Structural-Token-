# Catalyst Doctrine (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational doctrine draft. Not legal advice.

## Purpose
Define the non negotiable principles of Catalyst as a durable doctrine independent of any single project.

## Core principles
1) Compliance by architecture
- Controls are enforced by design, not by optional policy.

2) Real assets as value anchors
- Tokens represent economic rights tied to verifiable assets or revenues.

3) Collegial and auditable governance
- Decisions are ratified, recorded, and reconstructable.

4) Controlled geometric growth
- Expansion is staged through verified series activation and policy gates.

5) Structural limits to speculation
- Lockups, caps, and eligibility gates reduce runaway feedback loops.

6) Traceability as trust
- Evidence hashes and event trails support auditability.

7) Separation of layers
- Infrastructure is separate from projects and from actors.

## Doctrine boundaries
- No dependence on a single asset or founder.
- No promise of guaranteed returns.
- Long term operational continuity over short term liquidity.
