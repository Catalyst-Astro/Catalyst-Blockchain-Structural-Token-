# Institutional Adoption Playbook (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational guide. Not legal advice.

## Adoption modes
1) Internal implementation
- Single ecosystem deployment
- Full governance control

2) Licensed implementation
- Regional cooperatives or public programs
- Shared standard registry

3) Academic or experimental
- Controlled pilots with explicit scope
- Restricted listing and transfer phases

4) Hybrid public private
- Public infrastructure with private project layers

## Partnership pillars
- Compliance by design
- Independent auditability
- Transparent governance
- Long term operational continuity

## Long term stewardship
- Standard evolves by versioned governance.
- Doctrine remains anchored and accessible.
