# Certification and Audit Framework (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational framework. Not legal advice.

## Certification stages
1) Pre assessment
- Review core modules and interfaces.
- Verify governance and audit tooling.

2) Evidence review
- Validate hashes for doctrine, standard, and policies.
- Check audit trail integrity.

3) Conformity audit
- Test enforcement and policy activation.
- Validate incident response and freeze controls.

4) Certification issuance
- Record audit hash on chain.
- Activate certification with validity window.

## Recertification
- Required after major protocol changes.
- Required at fixed intervals defined by policy.

## Revocation
- DAO may revoke certification with reasonHash.
- Revocation event recorded on chain.

## Evidence registry (ASCII)
Doc hash -> Audit hash -> Certification record
