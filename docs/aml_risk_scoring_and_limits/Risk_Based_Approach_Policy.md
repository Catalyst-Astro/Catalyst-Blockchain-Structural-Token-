# Risk Based Approach Policy (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Objective
Apply proportional controls based on AML/CTF risk levels, instead of one-size-fits-all limits.

## Principles
- Risk is dynamic and must be refreshed on events and periodic reviews.
- Controls scale with risk (limits, approvals, and restrictions).
- No personal data is stored on-chain.

## Risk Levels
- LOW: normal operations with minimal constraints.
- MEDIUM: enforced caps and monitoring.
- HIGH: restricted actions and manual review.

## Governance
- Policies are versioned and activated by DAO_COUNCIL or RISK_ADMIN.
- Justifications for score changes are stored off-chain and hashed.
