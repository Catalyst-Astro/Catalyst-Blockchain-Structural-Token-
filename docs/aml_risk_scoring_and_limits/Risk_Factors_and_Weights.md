# Risk Factors and Weights (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Factors (Illustrative)
1) Jurisdiction risk (country of residence or entity).
2) User type (individual, corporate, institutional).
3) Cumulative investment amount.
4) Transaction frequency and velocity.
5) Corporate complexity (ties to UBO module).
6) External alerts (sanctions, PEP, adverse media).
7) On-chain behavior and anomaly flags.

## Example Weighting
- Jurisdiction: 30%
- User type: 15%
- Volume: 20%
- Frequency: 15%
- Corporate complexity: 10%
- Alerts: 10%

## Output
The off-chain engine produces:
- riskLevel (LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH)
- scoreVersion
- justification hash
