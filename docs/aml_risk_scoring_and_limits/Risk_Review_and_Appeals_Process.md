# Risk Review and Appeals Process (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Review Triggers
- Risk score expiration.
- Material change in ownership or control.
- High-risk alerts (sanctions, PEP, adverse media).
- Transaction anomalies or threshold breaches.

## Review Steps
1) Freeze or throttle actions if required by policy.
2) Collect supporting documents off-chain.
3) Compliance review and updated risk scoring.
4) Publish updated score hash and validity window.

## Appeals
- User submits appeal with evidence off-chain.
- Compliance team reviews and re-scores.
- On-chain score update if approved.
