# PEP and Sanctions Screening Policy (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Screening Sources
- OFAC and UN sanctions lists.
- EU restrictive measures.
- National PEP registries where available.

## Outcomes
- Match found: escalate to compliance review.
- High-risk: flag entity and restrict operations.
- Cleared: record evidence off-chain and update attestation hash.
