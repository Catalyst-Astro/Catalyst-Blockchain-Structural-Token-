# UBO Policy Advanced (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## UBO Thresholds
- Ownership >= 25% direct or indirect (configurable).
- Control via voting rights, agreements, or appointment powers.
- If no owner meets threshold, controlling administrators are treated as UBOs.

## Entity Types Covered
- SAS, SA de CV, SA, LLC equivalents.
- SPVs by project.
- Trust structures (fiduciary / beneficiaries / technical committee).
- Multi-layer holdings.

## Validity Rules
- UBO graph must be refreshed every N months.
- Immediate refresh triggered by ownership or control changes.
