# Corporate Document Checklist (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Core Documents
- Certificate of incorporation or constitution act.
- Shareholder registry / cap table.
- Powers of attorney and board resolutions.
- Trust deed (if applicable).

## UBO Evidence
- Beneficial owner IDs (off-chain).
- Ownership structure diagram.
- Signed UBO declaration.

## Refresh Triggers
- Ownership changes.
- New directors or controllers.
- Jurisdiction changes.
