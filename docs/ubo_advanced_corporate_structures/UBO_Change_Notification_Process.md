# UBO Change Notification Process (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Trigger Events
- Ownership transfer or dilution.
- New controlling shareholder or administrator.
- Trust committee changes.

## Steps
1) Notify compliance within 5 business days.
2) Submit updated corporate documents.
3) Provide updated UBO graph report.
4) Re-verify and update on-chain attestation.
