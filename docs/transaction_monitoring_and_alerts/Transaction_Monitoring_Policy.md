# Transaction Monitoring Policy (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Objective
Continuously monitor on-chain transactions and distributions to identify unusual or suspicious activity.

## Signals (Examples)
- Volume anomalies versus historical baselines or risk tier.
- Bursts and structuring (split transactions).
- Abrupt behavioral changes.
- Links to high-risk wallets or entities.
- Jurisdictional red flags.
- Repeated failed actions or limit bypass attempts.

## Governance
- Rules are versioned and activated by DAO_COUNCIL.
- Monitoring administrators propose new rules and thresholds.

## Privacy
- No personal data on-chain.
- Evidence is hashed and logged for auditability.
