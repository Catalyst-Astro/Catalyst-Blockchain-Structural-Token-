# Continuous Audit Framework (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Cadence
- Monthly internal review
- Quarterly external review (light)
- Annual full audit

## Audit Scope
- AML/CTF logs and alerts
- Governance decisions and execution
- Fiduciary revenue reconciliation
- Smart contract state changes

## Outputs
- Checkpoint IDs
- Evidence hashes
- Remediation plans
