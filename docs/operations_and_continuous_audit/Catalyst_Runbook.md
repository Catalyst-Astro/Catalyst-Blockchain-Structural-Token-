# Catalyst Runbook (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Daily Operations
- Monitor node health, RPC latency, and chain sync.
- Validate contract event ingestion and oracle freshness.
- Review compliance alerts and pending approvals.

## Roles
- Technical Ops: node health, uptime, incident response.
- Compliance Ops: AML alerts, freeze/unfreeze tracking.
- Fiduciary Ops: revenue reconciliation and distribution checks.
- Governance Ops: DAO agenda and execution coordination.

## SLAs (Illustrative)
- Critical incident response: 1 hour.
- Compliance review turnaround: 24-72 hours.
- Audit checkpoint cadence: monthly.
