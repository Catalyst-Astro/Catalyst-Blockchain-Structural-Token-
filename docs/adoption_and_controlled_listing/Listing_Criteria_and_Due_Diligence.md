# Listing Criteria and Due Diligence (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Mandatory criteria
- Venue has a documented KYC/AML program with audit evidence.
- Venue can enforce whitelist and travel rule requirements.
- Venue supports transfer restrictions, lockups, and freeze signals.
- Venue jurisdiction aligns with the active policy matrix.
- Venue provides compliance and incident escalation contacts.

## Due diligence checklist
- Regulatory status and licenses (hash recorded off chain).
- Ownership and governance structure.
- Compliance program summary and audit history.
- Technical integration test report.
- Incident response SLA and notification process.
- Reporting format and cadence for regulators and fiduciary.

## Decision record
- venueId and wallet
- approvalVersion
- decisionDate
- reasonHash (off chain evidence)
