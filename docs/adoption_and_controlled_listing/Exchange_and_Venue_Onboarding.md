# Exchange and Venue Onboarding (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Steps
1. Pre screen jurisdiction, license, and sanctions exposure.
2. Compliance review of KYC/AML, travel rule readiness, and reporting.
3. Technical integration test with listing and freeze controls.
4. Legal review of agreements and disclosures.
5. Pilot activation with caps and monitoring.
6. DAO activation of venue approval and phase.

## Required artifacts (hash only)
- License evidence hash
- Compliance policy hash
- Incident response SLA hash
- Integration test report hash

## Offboarding
- Revoke venue approval on chain.
- Notify fiduciary and compliance stakeholders.
- Record reasonHash and caseId off chain.
