# Regulatory Narrative Guidelines (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Allowed statements
- The token represents economic rights, not currency.
- Distribution is a private offering with eligibility checks.
- Compliance is enforced on chain and off chain.
- Transfers are restricted to verified wallets.

## Prohibited statements
- Guaranteed returns or yield.
- Open public sale or permissionless access.
- Marketing that implies a payment instrument.
- Claims that bypass eligibility or lockups.

## Review process
- Compliance review before public release.
- Record the approved message hash.
- Maintain a change log for updates.
