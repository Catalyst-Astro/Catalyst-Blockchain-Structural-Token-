# Token Legal Classification (Draft)

Disclaimer: This is an operational draft for internal alignment. It is not legal advice.

## Definition
Fractal Token represents an economic right linked to distributions from assets held under a trust structure. The token does not convey direct ownership of the underlying real estate or tourism assets. It represents a proportional right to distributions, subject to the trust and the operational rules defined off-chain.

## Trust Relationship
- The trust is the legal vehicle that holds assets and manages distributions.
- The token is a digital representation of a proportional economic right to those distributions.
- Token holders do not own the assets directly and do not control trust operations outside the governance process.

## Explicit Non-Characterization
The token is not:
- Legal tender or currency.
- A general-purpose payment instrument.
- A deposit, credit, or debt instrument.
- A derivative or guaranteed yield product.
- A promise of fixed returns.

## Compliance Positioning
The token is an RWA-linked economic right with transfer and eligibility restrictions. Access and transfer require compliance checks and acceptance of disclosures.
