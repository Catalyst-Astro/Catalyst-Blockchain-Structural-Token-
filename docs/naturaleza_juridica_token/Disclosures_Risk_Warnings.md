# Disclosures and Risk Warnings (Draft)

Disclaimer: This is an operational draft. It is not legal advice and does not guarantee regulatory compliance.

## Key Risks
- Market risk: asset values and tourism income can fluctuate.
- Execution risk: operational delays, cost overruns, or asset underperformance.
- Smart contract risk: code defects, integration failures, or security incidents.
- Regulatory risk: changes in law or enforcement may affect transferability or access.
- Liquidity risk: limited secondary market; transfers can be restricted or delayed.
- Force majeure: events beyond control that affect operations or distributions.

## No Guarantee of Returns
Token holders should not expect guaranteed or fixed returns. Distributions are conditional on asset performance and operational decisions under the trust.

## AML/KYC and Eligibility
Participation requires identity verification and acceptance of current disclosures. The issuer may suspend or restrict transfers to comply with legal and policy requirements.
