# Investor Suitability Policy (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Purpose
Define suitability and exposure limits based on risk profile and jurisdiction.

## Basic Profiling
- Retail: limited exposure, higher disclosure requirements.
- Accredited: higher limits, documented eligibility.
- Institutional: negotiated limits and reporting.

## Exposure Caps
- Maximum allocation per wallet or entity.
- Concentration limits by project or asset class.
- Periodic re-evaluation of limits based on activity.

## Jurisdiction Rules
Eligibility and limits are based on jurisdictional compliance rules. Wallets must be assigned a jurisdiction code off-chain and registered on-chain via the policy module.
