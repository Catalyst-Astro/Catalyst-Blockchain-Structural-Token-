# Whitelist Operating Policy (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Objective
Authorize wallets only after compliance checks and continuously enforce eligibility.

## Key Rules
- No wallet operates without whitelist approval.
- Suspensions are immediate upon compliance risk.
- Policies are versioned and auditable.

## Governance
- WHITELIST_ADMIN proposes approvals.
- COMPLIANCE_ADMIN can suspend or revoke.
- DAO_COUNCIL activates policy updates.
