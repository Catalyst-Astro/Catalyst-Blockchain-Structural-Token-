# Wallet Approval Checklist (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Pre-Approval Requirements
- KYC verified (STAR-04).
- Disclosures accepted (STAR-02).
- Eligibility confirmed (STAR-03).
- Risk score within policy thresholds (STAR-06).
- Travel Rule readiness where applicable (STAR-08).

## Evidence
Each approval should reference a reason hash pointing to off-chain records.
