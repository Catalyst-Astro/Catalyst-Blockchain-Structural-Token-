# Voting and Ratification Process (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Steps
1) Proposal created with description hash and optional caseId.
2) Committee members vote within the voting window.
3) Proposal is finalized and marked APPROVED/REJECTED/EXPIRED.
4) Approved proposals are executed via ComplianceExecutionBridge.

## Evidence
Votes, thresholds, and execution events are emitted on-chain.
