# Proposal Types and Quorums (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Proposal Types
- POLICY_APPROVAL
- FREEZE_RATIFICATION
- UNFREEZE_APPROVAL
- EXCEPTION_APPROVAL
- JURISDICTION_EXCEPTION
- EMERGENCY_CONFIRMATION

## Quorum Guidance
- Freeze and unfreeze: higher quorum and approval thresholds.
- Exceptions: require DAO_COUNCIL majority.
- Emergency: shortened voting period with ratification requirement.
