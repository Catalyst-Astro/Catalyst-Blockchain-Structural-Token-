# Incident Handling for Travel Rule (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Trigger Events
- Missing or rejected Travel Rule package.
- Counterparty verification failure.
- High-risk travel rule breach alerts.

## Response Steps
1) Escalate to compliance review.
2) Restrict or block transfers per policy.
3) Collect and hash supporting evidence.
4) Record resolution in Alert or Travel Rule evidence registries.
