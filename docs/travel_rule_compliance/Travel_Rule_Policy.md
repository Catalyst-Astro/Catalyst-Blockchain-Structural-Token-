# Travel Rule Policy (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Objective
Apply FATF Travel Rule controls to qualifying transfers while preserving privacy and auditability.

## When It Applies
- Transfers above the configured amount threshold.
- Transfers involving higher risk participants.
- Jurisdictional triggers defined by policy.

## Evidence
- TravelRulePackage.json is stored off-chain.
- On-chain evidence records only hashes and status.
