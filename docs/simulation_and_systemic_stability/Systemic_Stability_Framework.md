# Systemic Stability Framework (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational and research draft. Not legal advice.

## Goal
Provide a consistent framework to compare uncontrolled bubble dynamics against Catalyst geometric growth controls.

## Growth regimes
1) Arithmetic growth (linear)
- Typical for traditional real estate expansion.
- Capital inflow grows faster than asset absorption capacity.

2) Exponential growth (runaway)
- Typical for speculative markets without real asset anchors.
- Price diverges from fundamentals quickly.

3) Controlled geometric growth (Catalyst)
- Emission and activation tied to verified assets and series.
- Feedback loops dampen excess inflow.
- Lockups and caps reduce feedback amplification.

## Positive Malthusian Trap (definition)
- Not collapse, but structural limits that force capital to reorganize into productive flows.
- Limits are explicit, measurable, and governed by policy.

## Core assumptions
- Every series is backed by a real asset or revenue stream.
- Supply activation is staged and policy driven.
- Secondary market access is controlled and phased.

## Outputs
- Stability metrics for governance decisions.
- Evidence hashes for audit and disclosure.
