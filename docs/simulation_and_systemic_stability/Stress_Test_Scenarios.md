# Stress Test Scenarios (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational and research draft. Not legal advice.

## Scenario set
1) Classic real estate bubble
- rapid price growth disconnected from occupancy
- delayed corrections

2) Liquidity surge
- rapid inflows from external markets
- policy caps tested

3) Liquidity drain
- rapid outflows and reduced demand
- lockups and reserves tested

4) Regulatory shock
- temporary restrictions on listings or transfers
- freeze and listing controls tested

5) Multi series contagion
- simultaneous stress in multiple projects
- governance response tested

## Scenario outputs
- time to stabilization
- changes in SSI and BAI
- price vs asset value divergence
