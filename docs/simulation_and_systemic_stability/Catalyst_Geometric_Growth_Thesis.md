# Catalyst Geometric Growth Thesis (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational and research draft. Not legal advice.

## Thesis
Catalyst grows by controlled geometric expansion tied to real asset activation and policy governed listing phases.

## Mechanisms
- Series based issuance limits capital per project.
- Governance gates when a new series can be activated.
- Caps and lockups reduce positive feedback loops.
- Compliance gates reduce speculative velocity.

## Economic interpretation
- Capital growth is geometric but bounded by asset verification and revenue signals.
- Excess liquidity is absorbed by new series only after audit and policy approval.
- This reduces price decoupling from real asset value.

## Expected outcomes
- Lower volatility vs open markets.
- Slower formation of speculative bubbles.
- Faster correction after shocks due to policy throttles.
