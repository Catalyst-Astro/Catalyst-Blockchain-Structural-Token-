# DAO Decision Guided by Simulation (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational and research draft. Not legal advice.

## Decision inputs
- SSI and BAI from the latest snapshot.
- Price decouple index and volatility index.
- Correction time and recent stress results.

## Decision gates
- Activate new series only if SSI >= threshold.
- Raise caps only if BAI indicates absorption capacity.
- Slow listing phase progression under high volatility.

## Evidence
- Snapshot hash stored on chain.
- DAO proposal references the snapshot hash.
- Audit log links policy changes to simulation results.

## Suggested cadence
- Monthly snapshot update.
- Quarterly stress tests.
- Ad hoc updates after major shocks.
