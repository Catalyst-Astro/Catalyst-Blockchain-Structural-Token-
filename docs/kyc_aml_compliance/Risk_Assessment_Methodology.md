# Risk Assessment Methodology (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Inputs
- Jurisdiction risk level.
- Investor type (individual, entity, institutional).
- Transaction volume and frequency.
- Source of funds / economic activity (off-chain).

## Scoring
- LOW: standard monitoring.
- MEDIUM: enhanced monitoring.
- HIGH: restricted or blocked.

## Review Cadence
- LOW: annual.
- MEDIUM: quarterly.
- HIGH: continuous review and escalation.
