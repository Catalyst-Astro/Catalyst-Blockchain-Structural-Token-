# Suspension and Review Procedure (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Triggers
- Sanctions match or adverse media.
- High-risk score assignment.
- Suspicious activity alerts.

## Actions
- Immediate suspension on-chain.
- Notify compliance and legal stakeholders.
- Perform enhanced due diligence.

## Reinstatement
- Clear findings documented off-chain.
- Update risk score and identity status on-chain.
