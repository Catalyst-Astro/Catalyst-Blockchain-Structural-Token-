# AML/KYC Policy (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Purpose
Define AML/KYC controls for identifying participants, verifying identity, and preventing illicit activity while preserving privacy.

## Scope
- Individuals, legal entities, and institutional investors.
- All wallets that interact with Fractal Token distributions or transfers.

## Key Principles
- No anonymous participation.
- No personal data stored on-chain.
- Risk-based approach with periodic review.

## Compliance Obligations Covered
- Mexico: LFPIORPI and related AML obligations.
- FATF/GAFI recommendations.
- FinCEN (US) and MiCA (EU) principles where applicable.
