# Freeze and Incident Response Policy (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Objective
Provide a proportional, auditable mechanism to pause specific wallets, series, or functions during incidents.

## Freeze Types
- CRITICAL AML
- INCIDENT SECURITY
- LEGAL ORDER
- FIDUCIARY HOLD
- DISPUTE/LITIGATION

## Evidence
- Each freeze includes caseId and justificationHash.
- Unfreeze includes resolutionHash.
