# Catalyst Implementation Roadmap (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational roadmap. Not legal advice.

## Phase 1 - Institutional preparation (0-3 months)
- Define operating entity (SAS/SA/trust).
- Select base jurisdiction and regulatory counsel.
- Select pilot fiduciary or bank partner.
- Freeze Catalyst Core scope (mandatory modules).
- Finalize institutional whitepaper (STAR-15).
Success criteria:
- Core scope locked and versioned.
- Fiduciary pilot term sheet signed.
- Governance roles appointed.

## Phase 2 - Controlled pilot (3-6 months)
- Activate first project (single series).
- Onboard verified investors.
- Activate KYC, whitelist, SBT, restrictions.
- No secondary market.
- Execute first real distribution.
Success criteria:
- 100 percent transfers gated by compliance.
- First distribution reconciled with fiduciary evidence.
- No critical audit findings.

## Phase 3 - Validation and external audit (6-9 months)
- External technical and legal audit.
- Stress tests (STAR-18).
- Governance and policy tuning.
- Publish high level results (no sensitive data).
Success criteria:
- Critical findings = 0.
- Remediation plan approved for minors.

## Phase 4 - Controlled expansion (9-18 months)
- Activate new series and projects.
- Enable controlled secondary market.
- Add fiduciary or jurisdiction partners.
- Refine simulation and KPIs.
Success criteria:
- Secondary market caps enforced with zero bypass.
- SLA compliance for alerts and freezes.

## Phase 5 - Institutional scaling (18+ months)
- Institutional alliances.
- Territorial replication (STAR-19).
- Catalyst compatible certification.
- Academic or public standardization pilots.
Success criteria:
- At least one certified implementation.
- Replication pack published and audited.
