# Transfer Restrictions Policy (Draft)

Disclaimer: Operational draft. Not legal advice.

## Objective
Enforce private-offering and compliance restrictions for RWA token transfers.

## Policy Layers
- Series status (ACTIVE/PAUSED/CLOSED).
- Identity checks (whitelist or SBT).
- Disclosures acceptance.
- Eligibility for private offering.
- Lockups (series and wallet).
- Jurisdiction allow/deny lists.
- AML risk score thresholds.
- Travel Rule evidence where required.
